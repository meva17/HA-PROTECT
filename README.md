HA-PROTECT

High-Altitude Electrical & Electronic Equipment Protection System

About

HA-PROTECT is an ESP32-based system designed to monitor and protect electrical and electronic equipment in high-altitude environments.

The system monitors temperature, humidity, atmospheric pressure, and battery condition. It analyzes the sensor data and identifies the risk level, while controlling protection devices such as a heater or fan.

Hardware

* ESP32
* Temperature and humidity sensor
* Pressure sensor
* Battery monitoring sensor
* OLED display
* Heater
* Fan
